Common questions

My alarm did not ring

Open Settings › Dawnwick and check that Alarms & Timers is allowed. Without it, iOS will not let the app ring through Silent mode or a Focus. The app shows a warning banner on the Alarms tab when this permission is missing.

How do I stop the alarm?

By finishing the mission. That is the point of the app — there is no one-tap Stop. If you set a snooze limit, you can snooze until the limit is reached.

I cannot complete my mission

If a mission needs hardware or a permission that is unavailable — the camera for QR codes, the pedometer for steps — Dawnwick replaces it with a Math mission so you are never locked out. You can change the mission in the alarm editor.

Managing or cancelling Premium

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the renewal; Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Dawnwick › Settings › Restore Purchases.

Where is my sleep data stored?

On your iPhone. Dawnwick reads sleep from Apple Health and never uploads it. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the microphone record me?

No. Sleep noise monitoring measures how loud the room is, and nothing else. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The feature is optional.
