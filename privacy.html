Privacy Policy

Dawnwick · Last updated 6 September 2026

Dawnwick has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no advertising. Almost everything the app knows about you stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Your sleep, calendar, alarms, missions and streak never leave your device.
  • Your approximate location is sent to a weather service, and to nobody else.
  • Nothing is recorded from your microphone or camera.
  • We do not track you, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not sell data.

Permissions you can refuse

Every permission is optional except the alarm permission itself. If you refuse location, the weather card is hidden. If you refuse Health, sleep history is empty. If you refuse the microphone, noise monitoring is off. The app keeps working.

Children

Dawnwick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your data, your control

Because we hold nothing on our own servers, deleting the app removes everything Dawnwick stored about you. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are governed by their policies; write to us and we will request deletion of the RevenueCat record associated with your purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the new version will be published here with a new date, and noted in the app's release notes.
